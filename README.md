# Quang Nguyen

**ML research** · [Website](https://quangng2000.github.io/)

I focus on machine learning research. My projects explore program compilation into transformer components, speech restoration with pretrained audio representations, language-model steering with learned twisting functions and sequential Monte Carlo, and formal proofs of the mathematical foundations of learning.

## Research interests

- Program compilation and neuro-symbolic learning in transformers
- Speech restoration, audio representations, and mean-flow generative models
- Probabilistic inference and language-model steering with twisted SMC
- Probabilistic modeling and formal verification of machine learning foundations

## Selected work

| Project                                                                                              | Focus                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Cajal Compiler to Transformer Heads](https://github.com/quangng2000/riftco-transformer)             | Compiling typed Cajal-lite programs into programmed transformer components, including linear-attention heads, and combining them with learned components. |
| [TEMF + WavJEPA-Nat (FiLM)](https://github.com/quangng2000/temf-wavjepa-film)                        | A speech-restoration research prototype combining frozen WavJEPA-Nat features, FiLM conditioning, and TEMF mean-flow generation.                          |
| [SMC Steering with Learned Twisting Functions](https://quangng2000.github.io/projects/smc-steering/) | Inference-time language-model steering with contrastive twist learning, importance weighting, and adaptive SMC resampling.                                |
| [RMFSR](https://github.com/quangng2000/rmfsr)                                                        | An independent experimental implementation of MeanFlow speech restoration, with PyTorch models and training and evaluation tools.                         |
| [ELBO in Lean 4](https://github.com/quangng2000/lean-elbo)                                           | A Lean 4 formalization of the evidence lower bound for finite, strictly positive probability distributions.                                               |

More project notes and demonstrations are on my [website](https://quangng2000.github.io/).
